# The Ember Shrine

A round stone building at the top of the village, older than every
house around it. The [[hearth-ember]] sat in an iron bowl on the altar
for three hundred years. The bowl is cold now, and [[tobin]] keeps an
ordinary wood fire beside it that does nothing against the frost.

## Details

- The carvings around the walls tell the story of
  [[the-founding-flame]].
- Behind the altar, an iron hook where the carrying lantern always
  hung. It is empty.
- Small soot-black boot prints on the back step, too small for Tobin.
