# The Kettle and Coal

The village inn, run by [[maren]]. Low beams blackened by three
centuries of coal smoke, a hearth big enough to stand in, long tables
worn smooth by miners' elbows. Since the Ember went missing the fire is
kept roaring day and night, and people sleep on the benches rather than
go home to cold houses.

## Details

- The inn's well, in the yard, is the first in the village to freeze
  each night.
- A notice board by the door: Lodge shift lists, a lost-goat notice,
  and a torn page where the guild mark results were posted.
- The back window looks up the mine road toward the [[old-delve]].
