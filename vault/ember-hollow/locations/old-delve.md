# The Old Delve

The first mine of Ember Hollow, worked out a century ago and closed
when the new shafts opened lower down the valley. A timbered entrance
leads to a main gallery, a flooded lower level, and the old upper
forge where the first smiths worked iron straight from the rock.

## Details

- **The Frostmouth:** a crack at the bottom of the main gallery that
  breathes cold air. Frost spreads from it in feathered patterns.
- **The upper forge:** a side chamber with a chimney to the surface.
  On day 2 a thin line of smoke can be seen from the village.
- **Rime-crawlers** (see [[rime-crawlers]]) nest near the Frostmouth
  and hunt anything warm.
