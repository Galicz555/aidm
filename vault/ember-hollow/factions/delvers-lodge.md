# The Delvers' Lodge

The miners' and smiths' guild of Ember Hollow. It sets shifts, settles
disputes, and grants the guild mark that lets a smith sell work under
her own name. Its rules say an apprentice needs a living master to
vouch for her. [[gorrick]] leads it and keeps those rules exactly.
