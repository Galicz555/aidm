# Ember Hollow

A short classic-fantasy one-shot for one player, two to four hours of
play. Ember Hollow is a mining village of sixty souls high in the
Greyfold Hills. For three hundred years the [[hearth-ember]], a coal
that never burns out, has sat in the [[ember-shrine]] and kept the cold
of the deep mine where it belongs.

Three nights ago the Ember went missing.

Since then frost has crept up out of the [[old-delve]] in the middle of
summer: the wells skin over with ice by morning, breath smokes indoors,
and two goats have vanished from a locked pen. The [[delvers-lodge]]
wants to collapse the mine mouth and be done with it. The shrine keeper
[[tobin]] will not say where he was the night the Ember disappeared.

## Central tensions

- **Who took the Ember, and why.** It was not a thief from outside.
  The answer is someone the village likes, and the reason is pride, not
  malice.
- **Seal or search.** Foreman [[gorrick]] wants the mine sealed by dusk
  on day 2. If he does it, whoever is inside stays inside.
- **The cold is rising.** By the evening of day 3 the frost reaches the
  village square. After that, the Hollow is a place people leave.

## Opening scene

Evening, day one. The player character arrives at the
[[kettle-and-coal]] on the last cart up the hill road, cold to the bone
in what should be a warm summer dusk. The common room is full and quiet.
Everyone is sitting closer to the fire than they need to, and the
innkeeper [[maren]] is watching the door.
