# Ember Hollow quickstart rules

A light rule set for this one-shot. The DM calls for a roll only when
the outcome is uncertain and failure would be interesting.

## Character creation

1. **Name and concept.** One sentence: who you are and why you came up
   the hill road.
2. **Four abilities.** Assign +2, +1, +1 and 0 to Might, Grace, Wits and
   Heart.
3. **Two skills.** Pick two things you are trained in (for example
   climbing, lore, persuasion, smithing, stealth, healing). A trained
   skill adds +2 to a roll.
4. **Hit points.** 10 + Might.
5. **Gear.** Travelling clothes, a pack, a light, one weapon, and one
   personal item with a story.

## Rolling

Roll a d20 and add an ability and, if it applies, a skill bonus. The
DM reads the total:

- 1 on the die: something goes wrong beyond the failure.
- 2-9: failure or a complication.
- 10-14: success at a cost.
- 15-19: clean success.
- 20 on the die: success with a bonus.

## Harm

Most hits cost 1-4 hit points. At 0 the character is down and out of
the scene, not dead, unless the story has made death the stakes.
