# Rowan

A travelling tinker who mends pots, lamps and locks from village to
village. Rowan came up the hill road because Ember Hollow is famous for
its lamp-glass and its fire that never goes out, and arrived the week
it went out.

## Psychology (HEXACO)

- **Honesty-Humility (4):** Charges fair prices and says so.
- **Emotionality (3):** Calm on the road; uneasy underground.
- **Extraversion (3):** Friendly in an inn, quiet on a job.
- **Agreeableness (4):** Prefers a fix that leaves everyone talking.
- **Conscientiousness (4):** Keeps tools oiled and promises kept.
- **Openness (5):** Cannot leave a mystery or a strange mechanism
  alone.

## Abilities

- Might +0, Grace +1, Wits +2, Heart +1
- Skills: tinkering, persuasion

## Inventory

- Tinker's pack: files, pliers, lamp wicks, a small hammer.
- A bright brass lantern of Rowan's own make.
- A walking staff.
- A letter of introduction from a glassmaker in the lowlands.

## Goals

- **Buy lamp-glass** from the Hollow's workshop and move on before the
  autumn rains.
- **Find out what the Ember really was.** Rowan has heard the stories
  all the way down the valley.

## Fears

- **Tight dark tunnels.** A cave-in on the road years ago left Rowan
  sweating in any passage lower than a doorway.
