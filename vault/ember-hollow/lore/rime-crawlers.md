# Rime-crawlers

Pale, many-legged cave hunters the length of a forearm, drawn out of
the deep by the cold of the Frostmouth. They are blind and hunt by
warmth. Alone they are a nuisance; a swarm can drag down a goat.

- They hate bright light and the ring of struck metal, and scatter from
  both for a few moments.
- Fire keeps them at a distance but draws more of them to it.
- When the Ember returns to the shrine and the Frostmouth closes, they
  go back to the deep.
