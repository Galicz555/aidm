# The Founding Flame

The first miners of the Greyfold dug too deep and opened the
Frostmouth, a crack that breathes the cold of the deep earth. Their
smith, a woman the carvings call Hessa the Bright, went down with a
lantern and came back with a fire spirit that had lived in the rock. It
agreed to sleep in the village shrine and keep the cold below, as long
as it was kept and honoured.

The story says the spirit wakes if it is taken from the shrine for more
than three nights, and that a waking spirit chooses where it wants to
burn.
