## Campaign tone: Ember Hollow

### Genre

Classic fantasy, small scale. A village problem with a mystery at the
centre, a little danger in the dark, and people worth saving.

### Register

Warm, grounded, a touch wry. The villagers are practical folk who
joke to keep the cold out. Wonder is allowed: the Ember is real magic,
and the mine is old and strange.

### Danger

Fights are short and have weight, but nobody here is cruel. The
rime-crawlers are animals, not villains. Injuries hurt; death is
possible but never the point.

### Content

Suitable for a general audience. No gore, no sexual content.
Friendship, guilt and forgiveness carry the emotional weight.
