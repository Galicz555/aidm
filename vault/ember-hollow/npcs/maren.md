# Maren Ashby

**Gloss:** Innkeeper of the [[kettle-and-coal]], fifties, flour on her
sleeves and a ledger in her apron. She hears everything that happens in
the Hollow, usually before it has finished happening.

## Psychology (HEXACO)

- **Honesty-Humility (4):** Fair in trade, honest with strangers. Will
  shade the truth to protect a friend.
- **Emotionality (3):** Steady in a crisis; cries afterwards, in the
  cellar, where nobody sees.
- **Extraversion (5):** Talks to everyone, remembers every name, keeps
  the common room alive by force of will.
- **Agreeableness (4):** Slow to judge, quick to feed people.
- **Conscientiousness (4):** Runs a tight house; knows exactly how much
  firewood is left and how many nights it will last.
- **Openness (3):** Trusts old ways. The Ember was always there; that
  was the point of it.

## Goals

- **Object:** Keep the village together until the Ember is found.
- **Obstacle:** She suspects [[edda]] knows something, and she is not
  ready to say so out loud.
- **Time pressure:** The inn's woodpile lasts until day 3.

## Relationships

- [[edda]]: like a niece to her. Edda has not come for supper in three
  days, and Maren has told nobody.
- [[tobin]]: an old friend she is worried about.
- [[gorrick]]: respects him, thinks sealing the mine is a mistake.

## Knowledge

- Edda was turned down for her guild mark by the Lodge a week ago and
  took it badly.
- The goat pen that was emptied is the one nearest the mine road.
