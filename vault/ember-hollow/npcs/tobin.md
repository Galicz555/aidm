# Brother Tobin

**Gloss:** Keeper of the [[ember-shrine]], seventy and stooped, the
last of a line of keepers who have watched the [[hearth-ember]] for
three centuries. He fell asleep on watch the night it vanished and has
not forgiven himself.

## Psychology (HEXACO)

- **Honesty-Humility (5):** Cannot lie well and does not try. He simply
  stops talking.
- **Emotionality (4):** Carries the guilt visibly; flinches when the
  Ember is mentioned.
- **Extraversion (2):** Prefers the quiet of the shrine to company.
- **Agreeableness (5):** Will forgive anyone anything, except himself.
- **Conscientiousness (2):** The shrine is tidy, his memory is not. He
  has been tired for a long time.
- **Openness (4):** Knows the old lore better than anyone and is
  curious about what the Ember really is.

## Goals

- **Object:** See the Ember back in its bowl before the cold reaches
  the village.
- **Obstacle:** Admitting he slept on watch would cost him the shrine.
- **Time pressure:** He believes the shrine's wards fail on the third
  night without the Ember.

## Relationships

- [[maren]]: an old friend; she brings him soup he does not eat.
- [[edda]]: he taught her letters when she was small. He heard her
  voice in the shrine that night and told himself he dreamed it.

## Knowledge

- The Ember is not a coal but a sleeping fire spirit bound by the
  village founders (see [[the-founding-flame]]).
- The Ember must be carried in the iron lantern kept behind the altar,
  or it burns whoever holds it. The lantern is missing too.
