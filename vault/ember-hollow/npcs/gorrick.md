# Gorrick Vane

**Gloss:** Foreman of the [[delvers-lodge]], fifty, broad as a door
frame. He has buried miners and does not intend to bury more. If the
cold comes from the mine, the mine gets sealed.

## Psychology (HEXACO)

- **Honesty-Humility (4):** Means what he says; expects the same.
- **Emotionality (2):** Hard to rattle. Grief comes out as work.
- **Extraversion (3):** Speaks when he has decided something.
- **Agreeableness (2):** Not cruel, but not easily moved.
- **Conscientiousness (5):** Has already measured the powder for the
  collapse charge.
- **Openness (2):** Does not believe in fire spirits. Believes in
  rock, air and cold.

## Goals

- **Object:** Seal the [[old-delve]] mouth before the cold spreads.
- **Obstacle:** Half the village thinks the answer is inside the mine.
- **Time pressure:** He sets the charge at dusk on day 2.

## Relationships

- [[edda]]: refused her mark by the Lodge's rules, and privately thinks
  she is the best smith in the hills.
- [[maren]]: drinks at her inn, listens to her more than he admits.

## Knowledge

- The upper forge in the old workings still has a working chimney.
- The collapse charge would bring down the whole entrance, upper
  forge included.
