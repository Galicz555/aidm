# Edda Flint

**Gloss:** Nineteen, apprentice to the village smith who died last
winter. Talented, stubborn, and refused her guild mark by the
[[delvers-lodge]] because her master was not alive to vouch for her.
She took the [[hearth-ember]] to forge a blade good enough that the
Lodge could not say no.

## Psychology (HEXACO)

- **Honesty-Humility (3):** Proud. Meant to put the Ember back by
  morning and tell nobody.
- **Emotionality (4):** Frightened now, and furious with herself for
  it.
- **Extraversion (3):** Talks freely about metal, not about feelings.
- **Agreeableness (3):** Argues first, apologises later.
- **Conscientiousness (5):** Meticulous at the forge; the one careless
  thing she ever did was this.
- **Openness (5):** Fascinated by the Ember. She has learned more about
  it in three days than the shrine has in a century.

## Goals

- **Object:** Get out of the [[old-delve]] alive and return the Ember
  before anyone finds out it was her.
- **Obstacle:** The rime-crawlers have her trapped in the upper forge;
  the Ember is the only heat in the mountain, and they want it.
- **Time pressure:** Her food ran out yesterday.

## Relationships

- [[maren]]: the closest thing she has to family.
- [[gorrick]]: the man who refused her mark. She wants to prove him
  wrong more than she wants to be safe.
- [[tobin]]: she took the Ember while he slept and feels sick about it.

## Knowledge

- Rime-crawlers hate bright light and loud metal (see
  [[rime-crawlers]]).
- She has the iron lantern; the Ember is safe to carry in it.
