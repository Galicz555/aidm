# The Hearth Ember

A fist-sized coal that glows a steady orange and gives off the heat of
a full forge. It is a fire spirit asleep, bound by the village founders
(see [[the-founding-flame]]). While it sits in the shrine the cold of
the [[old-delve]] stays below ground. Carried outside the iron lantern,
it burns anyone who touches it.
