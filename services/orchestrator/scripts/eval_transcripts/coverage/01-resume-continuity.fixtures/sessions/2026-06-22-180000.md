# Session Log — The Crooked Sparrow

Rowan reached *The Crooked Sparrow* just past dusk, the rain still
hammering the bad streets of the Old District. The inn was crowded
for a Tuesday — too crowded.

**Marin**, the barkeep, recognized Rowan from the previous week's
detour through her quarter. She poured him an ale without asking and
nodded at the corner booth where a man with a torn cloak sat alone.
"That's Hadrik. Talks to no one. Watch his hands."

Hadrik had a map. He didn't show it, but Rowan saw the edge of paper
slip from under his sleeve once. When Rowan approached, Hadrik
mistook him for a hired blade — said the words "the Veil's collectors
won't find me here", then clammed up when he realised Rowan wasn't
who he expected.

Outside, the rain started turning to sleet. Marin closed the shutters
herself, slid the bar across the back door. Something was coming
through the Old District tonight, and the Crooked Sparrow was where
people went when they didn't want to be found.

**Open threads:**
- Hadrik's map — what is it of, and who are "the Veil's collectors"?
- Marin clearly knows more than she's saying about both.
- The session ended with Rowan still seated, Hadrik still in his booth.
