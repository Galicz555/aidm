# Marin the Barkeep

Late-fifties, broad shoulders, hair shot through with grey. Runs *The
Crooked Sparrow* alone. Knows everyone in town by their drink, not by
their name. Watches more than she talks.

## Psychology (HEXACO)

- **Honesty-Humility (4):** Plain dealer.
- **Emotionality (3):** Steady.
- **Extraversion (2):** Quiet. The bar speaks for her.
- **Agreeableness (3):** Civil but not warm.
- **Conscientiousness (4):** Counts the till twice.
- **Openness (2):** Cares about her town. Little else.
