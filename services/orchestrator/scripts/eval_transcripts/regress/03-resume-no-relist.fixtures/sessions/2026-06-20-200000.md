# Session Log

Rowan left the abandoned warehouse before dawn and pushed deeper into
the Old District in search of medical supplies. The rain made every
alley sound the same. He found a looted pharmacy, *Pharmacia Lux*, and
slipped inside before a patrol could spot him.

In the wreckage of the back shelves he found Lyra — a young woman with
a jagged scar from temple to jaw, gaunt and pale, hiding from people
she would only call "the collectors." A man named Kael followed them
into the pharmacy: scarred, eyes glowing faintly, claiming Lyra was
"his." Rowan killed Kael with a thrown knife and shielded Lyra.

They hid in the back room as Kael's people searched the front. The
pursuers eventually gave up. Lyra patched a shallow cut on Rowan's
forearm — she barely trusted him, but had nowhere else to go.

Open threads: Kael's faction (the "collectors") will come looking for
their missing man. Lyra knows more about them than she's said.
