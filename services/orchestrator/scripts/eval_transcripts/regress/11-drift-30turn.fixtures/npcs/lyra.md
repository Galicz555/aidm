# Lyra

A road-weary leatherworker in her late twenties. Wears a coat of
patched oilcloth and carries a small tin whistle on a cord around
her neck — the only thing she has left from her sister. She works
the abandoned warehouse because the back stove still draws, and
because nobody comes here. She does not trust strangers, but she
has not forgotten how to be civil to one.

## Psychology (HEXACO)

- **Honesty-Humility (3)**: Will lie about her trade if pressed.
  Otherwise plain-spoken.
- **Emotionality (5)**: Jumps at sudden sounds. Hears footsteps
  through three walls.
- **Extraversion (2)**: Speaks in short sentences, lets silence do
  the rest of the work.
- **Agreeableness (4)**: Offers tea before she offers her name.
- **Conscientiousness (4)**: Knows where every loose floorboard
  creaks.
- **Openness (4)**: Reads people quickly; trusts her read.
